# Canonical object, evidence and claim contract

Stable identity is separated from mutable version. A country-platform operating scope is a distinct canonical operating object and may link to a parent product family. Every version has `valid_from`, optional `valid_to`, `as_of_time`, an attribute hash and at most one current effective state.

Evidence source, processing status, claim state and conclusion grade are separate:

- source: public observation, authorized first/third party, official rule, professional opinion, benchmark, synthetic fixture or user assertion;
- processing: raw, normalized, verified, conflicted, stale or invalid;
- claim: hypothesis, proposed, validated, rejected, blocked, inconclusive or superseded;
- grade: descriptive, associational, predictive, attributed, causal or decision-grade.

Verified evidence does not validate a claim. A validated claim does not prove causality. Attributed outcomes are not incremental without qualified counterfactual evidence. Synthetic fixtures support testing only. Every claim records object, time, owner, evidence dependencies, allowed uses and forbidden uses.

Missing, unavailable, not applicable, not yet observed, withheld, invalid and observed zero are distinct states. Missing values never silently become zero.
